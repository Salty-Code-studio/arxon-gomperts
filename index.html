WHO I AM

BUILT BY
THE WIND

Arxon Gomperts is a professional windsurfer specializing in fin slalom racing. Driven by a relentless passion for speed and technical mastery, Arxon has been pushing his limits on international waters since a young age.

Powering a fully loaded slalom sail through tactical buoy racing and blazing downwind runs, Arxon brings raw commitment, precision, and a hunger to reach the top of the sport.
