FAQ

Good to know.

How can I sponsor Arxon?

Get in touch through the form above, by email at arxon.nb2011@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +599 788 8046. His manager Xavi Werleman will walk you through equipment, kit and content options.

Which events does Arxon race?

The PWA Youth World Tour — Aruba Hi-Winds, the Bonaire PWA Youth Slalom World Cup and European stops such as Alaçatı (Turkey) and Cold Hawaii (Denmark) — plus IFCA fin slalom championships.

What discipline does he race?

Fin slalom is his main discipline: he is a two-time PWA U15 Fin Slalom World Champion and races U17 from 2026. He also trains foil, wing and freestyle.

Where does he train?

At Frans Paradise on Lac Bay (Sorobon), Bonaire, in the training group of Bonaire PWA legend Taty Frans.

Who are his current partners?

Arxon is backed by 18 partners from Bonaire, the Caribbean and beyond, led by his equipment partners North Sails (sails) and Future Fly (boards). See them all on the sponsorship page.

Where can I follow him?

On Instagram at @arxon_nb2011, and on this site's news page for race reports.
