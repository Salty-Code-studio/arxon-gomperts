Dominant home win at the 2026 Bonaire PWA Youth Slalom World Cup

Racing on his home water at Lac Bay, Arxon took the U17 Boys Fin title after winning 11 of the 16 eliminations sailed.

Arxon at Lac Bay during the 2026 Bonaire PWA Youth Slalom World Cup · Photo © PWA World Tour

The PWA's official final-day report from Lac Bay confirms Arxon (Future Fly / North Sails) as the U17 Boys Fin winner at the 2026 Bonaire PWA Youth Slalom World Cup. Over four days he won 11 of the 16 eliminations, a margin the PWA called well deserved.

Behind him the battle for the podium went down to the last heats: Thomas Michael claimed second with a late elimination win, and Trevor Antony held third by just 2.1 points over Adolfo Fernandez. The PWA's Day 1 report notes that after a slower opening heat Arxon reeled off seven wins in a row.

SURF magazine's wrap-up of the event highlights the same consistency, describing a rider who left the fleet fighting over places two to four.

Key facts

  • U17 Boys Fin — 1st, Bonaire 2026
  • 11 of 16 eliminations won
  • Podium: Gomperts, Thomas Michael, Trevor Antony
  • Seven straight heat wins on day 1
